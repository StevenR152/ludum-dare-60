You're a young fox helping an old owl master potter who can't see anymore. He still throws the pots on his kick wheel. You do everything else: dig the clay, glaze it, fire it in the kiln. Take your time, it's a chill game with a few tense moments.

Controls

  • Arrow keys or WASD: walk the fox around, inside and outside
  • Mouse click: work the thing you're standing at (door, owl, kiln). Click the ground, or a station out of reach, to walk there
  • Hold the mouse button on a clay patch: dig
  • E: pick which item in your pack is selected
  • Space: act on what you're touching, like a click (hold to dig, wedge or chop)
  • Backspace: drop the selected item where you stand. Click it to pick it up again, even from across the room; walking into a dropped pot can crack it
  • Touch screens: touch and hold to walk toward your finger, tap to act

Things only work while the fox is touching them, so walk up first. Your pack holds 2 things in any mix, and the clay you carry slows you down.

How to play

Make a pot from start to finish:

  1. Dig clay. Stand on a clay patch outside and hold the mouse button for 2 seconds. The patch greys out and comes back after a while.
  2. Weigh the clay. Go through the door and click the weighing station (the wooden tray of scales) with a dug kilo selected, then click it again to weigh off the grams the order needs.
  3. Wedge the clay. Stand on the workbench with the weighed clay selected and hold the mouse button for 3 seconds. The clay gets a white outline once it's wedged. Then click a model on the bench to carry it.
  4. Deliver the clay. Click the owl while touching him. He only takes wedged clay with a model. He takes it when he's idle and throws a pot on his kick wheel (about 8 seconds).
  5. Collect the pot. When his pot is ready, click him with a free slot to carry it.
  6. Dry the pot. Click the drying shelf with the pot selected to put it on (3 places). It dries from wet to leather hard to bone dry, and a bar under it shows how far it has got; click the shelf again to take it off. Hand the master a leather-hard pot and he'll trim it. Only a bone-dry pot survives the kiln.
  7. Gather wood. Hold the mouse button on the trees to the left to chop logs (you carry 2). The kiln holds 3, and how many you load sets the heat: 1 log low, 2 mid, 3 high.
  8. Fire the pot (bisque). Outside, click the kiln to load the pot and the logs, then click the torch beside it to light the fire. Each clay wants its own heat (black low, red mid, white high), and a pot fired at the wrong one cracks as it cools. It fires, then cools; click the kiln again to take the pot out.
  9. Dip in glaze. Inside, click a glaze bucket with the fired pot selected to dip it. An order may want one glaze, both, or none, and when it wants both, the order you dip them in matters.
  10. Fire again. Every pot goes back in the kiln for a second firing, glazed or not. This one can be at any heat; it just needs wood. A small flame badge on each pot shows whether it has been fired once or twice.
  11. Complete the order. Click the completed shelf to put the finished pot on it. A pot that matches an open order (shape, clay and glaze) is delivered on the spot. Fill every order on the list to finish the mission and open the next one.
