You're a young fox helping an old owl master potter who can't see anymore. He still throws the pots on his kick wheel. You do everything else: dig the clay, glaze it, fire it in the kiln. Take your time, it's a chill game with a few tense moments.

Controls

  • Arrow keys: walk the fox around, inside and outside
  • Mouse click: work the thing you're standing at (door, owl, kiln)
  • Hold the mouse button on a clay patch: dig
  • E: pick which clay in your backpack is selected
  • Space: drop the selected item where you stand. Click it to pick it up again; walking into a dropped pot can crack it
  • Touch screens: touch and hold to walk toward your finger, tap to drop

Things only work while the fox is touching them, so walk up first. You carry up to 2 clays in your backpack (each one slows you down) and one thing in your hands.

How to play

Make a pot from start to finish:

  1. Dig clay. Stand on a clay patch outside and hold the mouse button for 2 seconds. The patch greys out and comes back after a while.
  2. Weigh the clay. Go through the door and click the purple weighing station with a dug kilo selected, then click it again to weigh off the grams the order needs.
  3. Wedge the clay. Stand on the workbench with the weighed clay selected and hold the mouse button for 3 seconds. The clay gets a white outline once it's wedged. Then click a model on the bench to carry it.
  4. Deliver the clay. Click the owl while touching him. He only takes wedged clay with a model. He takes it when he's idle and throws a pot on his kick wheel (about 8 seconds).
  5. Collect the pot. When his pot is ready, click him with empty hands to carry it.
  6. Dry the pot. Click the drying shelf with the pot selected to put it on (3 places). It dries for 15 seconds and turns paler; click the shelf again to take a dried pot. The kiln won't take a wet pot.
  7. Gather wood. Coming soon The wood kiln needs fuel before it will fire.
  8. Fire the pot (bisque). Outside, click the kiln with the pot in your hands. It fires for 10 seconds, cools for 10 more, then click it with empty hands to collect the fired pot.
  9. Dip in glaze. Coming soon Pick the glaze bucket that matches the master's order and dip the pot for the right length of time.
  10. Fire again. Coming soon A second firing melts the glaze into its final colour.
  11. Complete the order. Click the completed shelf with a fired pot to put it on. Three completed pots end the game. Coming soon Matching each pot to the master's order, with glaze.
